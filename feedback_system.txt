🚀 HOW TO USE:

1. Click any photo in the gallery
2. Modal opens with EXIF data on left, photo on right
3. In the feedback section, click stars to rate (1-5)
4. Optionally add a comment in the textarea
5. Click "📝 Submit Feedback" button
6. See updated statistics instantly
7. View rating distribution and average
